      How to play
      • Move: joystick (or WASD / arrows). Up/down moves you deeper or closer on the court.
      • SHOOT: hold, let go in the green. Green = bucket, every time (except half-court heaves). The green shrinks when you're contested, fading, or shooting on the run, and if you're smothered there's no green at all. Run at the rim and press it to dunk.
      • CROSS = dribble moves (pick with the joystick): sideways = crossover · toward the rim with a defender on you = spin · away from the rim = stepback · no stick = hesi (freezes a defender who lunges). Shooting right out of a stepback or hesi is IN RHYTHM = bigger green.
      • PASS to a teammate (aim with the joystick). In 1v1 it's a pump fake.
      • Defense: BLOCK = jump into the ball while it's going up. STEAL = reach for the ball (get near the dribble). SLIDE = quick step to cut off a drive.
      • STEAL is a reach — the ball handler can CROSS during it to dodge and leave you shook.
      • Hit 3 in a row and you're ON FIRE 🔥 — bigger green until you miss.
      • 16-second shot clock. Ball out of bounds = other team's ball.
      • Keyboard: WASD move · hold Space shoot·block · K pass·steal · L cross·slide · 1–8 emotes.
